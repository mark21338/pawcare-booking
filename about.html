About PawCare

We make it simple for pet owners to find and book good animal care.

Our mission

Booking care for an animal should not mean long phone calls or waiting for replies. PawCare puts services, availability, and booking in one place so owners can plan ahead and animals get the attention they need.

Animals we care for

  • Dogs and puppies
  • Cats and kittens
  • Rabbits, hamsters, and other small pets
  • Birds

What to prepare before a visit

  • Bring vaccination records, especially for boarding and grooming.
  • Tell us about allergies, medicines, and any behavior concerns.
  • Use a carrier for cats and small pets, and a leash for dogs.
  • Arrive a few minutes early so your pet can settle in.
